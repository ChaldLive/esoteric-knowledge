# Law of Attraction

The second of three cosmic laws in TCF. Governs [[solar-fire]] — the fire of mind and soul. Division F of Section Two is devoted to it.

## Definition

The Law of Attraction draws together that which belongs together. It is the law of the Second Aspect — Love-Wisdom. Where the [[law-of-economy]] governs the behavior of matter in isolation, the Law of Attraction governs the drawing of forms into relationship.

## Its Operation

- At the atomic level: the force that draws atoms into molecules and forms
- At the human level: the force of love, desire, and affinity
- At the soul level: the magnetic pull of the higher self on the lower
- At the cosmic level: the attraction of solar systems toward each other; the force that will eventually draw all back to the Logos

## Attraction and [[Manas]]

Mind (manas) works through attraction — the mind draws in ideas, perceives relationships, builds conceptual structures. The [[solar-fire]] expressing through manas is fundamentally an attractive force, as opposed to the dispersive tendency of the [[law-of-economy]].

## The Love-Wisdom Identification

Bailey identifies this law with love — not sentiment, but the fundamental cohesive force of the universe. The Solar Logos being on the second Ray of Love-Wisdom means the Law of Attraction is the dominant law of this solar system.
