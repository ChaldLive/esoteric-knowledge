# Macrocosm and Microcosm

The law of correspondence — "as above, so below" — is the structural method of TCF. Every principle that applies at the cosmic scale applies isomorphically at the solar, planetary, and human scales. Bailey uses this not as metaphor but as an actual claim about the structure of reality.

## The Principle in TCF

> "The Stanzas of Dzyan... are simply extensions of the three fundamentals to be found in the Proem in the first volume of the Secret Doctrine by H.P. Blavatsky."

The three fires repeat at every scale:
- Cosmos: Electric Fire / Solar Fire / Fire by Friction as three cosmic aspects
- Solar system: Will / Love-Wisdom / Active Intelligence as Logoic qualities
- Planet: the same triad, expressing through the Planetary Logos
- Human: [[monad]] / soul / personality
- Atom: positive nucleus / electrons / atomic motion

## The Nested Hierarchy

| Scale | Entity | Vehicle |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Cosmic | Cosmic Logos | A constellation |
| Solar | [[The-logos\|Solar Logos]] | The solar system |
| Planetary | Planetary Logos | A planet |
| Human | The human being | A physical body |
| Atomic | The atom | Atomic structure |

Each level is simultaneously a microcosm of the one above and a macrocosm of the one below.

## Practical Implications

For Bailey's method, this means that understanding any level illuminates all others. The study of the atom reveals the structure of the solar system. The understanding of human initiation illuminates the initiation of a Planetary Logos. The fires in the human spine correspond to cosmic fires.

## Source in the Hermetic Tradition

The principle connects TCF to the Hermetic tradition (the Emerald Tablet, Paracelsus, the Kabbalah's four worlds) and to Blavatsky's *Secret Doctrine* which is its immediate source.
