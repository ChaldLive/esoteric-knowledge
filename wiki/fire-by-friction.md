# Fire by Friction

The third of [[the-three-fires]]. Corresponds to Matter, the Third Aspect, Active Intelligence. It is the fire inherent in physical substance itself. Section One of TCF covers it through six divisions.

## Core Characteristics

- The fire latent in all matter; the motion of atoms
- Produces heat through friction — hence the name
- Its law is the [[law-of-economy]]
- At the human level it manifests as [[kundalini]] — the fire at the base of the spine
- Governs the three lower vehicles: physical, astral, mental (lower)

## The Internal Fires of the Sheaths

Every sheath (physical, astral, mental) has its own internal fire — the animating heat of that body. These fires are related to the centers (chakras), and their awakening and regulation is a key concern.

## Fire by Friction and the Elementals

The [[fire-elementals]] (Agnichaitans) are the builders and animators of the physical plane. They work through Fire by Friction. Their activity is one of the hidden keys to understanding physical-plane phenomena.

## Relationship to Kundalini

[[Kundalini]] is the concentrated, latent form of Fire by Friction in the human system. Its arousal — always under the influence of [[solar-fire]] — is part of the initiatory process. Premature arousal is explicitly warned against.
