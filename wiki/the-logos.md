# The Logos

The divine intelligence behind a system of manifestation. TCF operates with a nested hierarchy of Logoi — each a living cosmic entity whose body of expression is a physical system at its corresponding scale.

## The Hierarchy of Logoi

| Level | Entity | Body of Expression |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| Cosmic | Cosmic Logos | A constellation / group of solar systems |
| Solar | Solar Logos | The solar system |
| Planetary | Planetary Logos | A planet (e.g. Earth = the "Earth Logos") |
| Human | Individual | The human physical body |

This is the core expression of [[macrocosm-microcosm]]: the same triadic structure of [[the-three-fires]] is operative at every level.

## The Solar Logos

The Solar Logos is described as a cosmic mental entity — a being whose consciousness operates on the cosmic mental plane. The solar system is literally its body, the seven planes being its vehicles. Its "soul" is [[solar-fire]]; its spirit, [[electric-fire]].

The evolution of our solar system is the evolution of the Solar Logos — it is working out a purpose that is, from our standpoint, unknowable.

## The Planetary Logos

Each planet is the body of a Planetary Logos. The seven sacred planets correspond to the seven Rays (see [[the-rays]]). The non-sacred planets are at an earlier stage of development. The Planetary Logos of Earth is said to be at a particular point in its initiatory unfoldment.

## The Logos and [[Manas]]

The entire solar system is described as the mental plane product of the Solar Logos's thought. Creation is a thought-form of the Logos — which gives [[thought-elementals]] and [[fire-elementals]] their ultimate cosmic context.
