# Fire Elementals (Agnichaitans)

The living entities that constitute and animate the fire of the physical plane. Known in TCF as Agnichaitans — a Sanskrit term meaning roughly "fire-lives." Division D of Section Two treats them alongside [[thought-elementals]].

## Nature

- Deva (angelic) lives of the fire element
- Their substance *is* [[fire-by-friction]] — they are not beings who use this fire, they *are* it
- Subdivided by scale: from atomic fire-lives up through large deva intelligences governing entire planes

## Classification

Bailey gives a broad classification of deva lives from the most elemental upward:
- **Sub-atomic Agnichaitans**: the fire in atomic substance itself
- **Agnichaitans of the physical plane**: the builders and animators of physical form
- **Greater Agnichaitans**: deva intelligences governing regions of the etheric plane

## Relationship to the [[Etheric-Body]]

The etheric plane is the domain where Agnichaitans are most directly encountered in human experience. The [[etheric-body]] is built and maintained by these lives; health and disease are partially a function of their activity.

## The Devas and Human Evolution

TCF describes two great streams of evolution proceeding in parallel: the human (self-conscious) stream and the deva (form-building) stream. They are interdependent — humans provide the directing thought; devas provide the substance and the building. Conscious cooperation with deva lives is part of the future of human spiritual development.
