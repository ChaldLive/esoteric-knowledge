# Esoteric Knowledge Base

This library rests on two primary Bailey texts that form an inseparable pair:

- **[[the-three-fires|*A Treatise on Cosmic Fire*]]** (1925) — the cosmological skeleton: the three fires, the planes, the rays, the Monad, the egoic lotus. Maps the structure of existence.
- **[[rays-and-initiations|*The Rays and the Initiations*]]** (1960) — the same structure animated from the disciple's standpoint: the nine initiations, the Science of the [[antahkarana|Antahkarana]], Shamballa, group initiation. Keeps the promises TCF defers.

Neither book is fully intelligible without the other. TCF provides the framework; R&I is the framework experienced from within.
