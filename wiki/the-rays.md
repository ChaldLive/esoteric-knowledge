# The Rays

The seven rays are the seven qualitative differentiations of divine life — the seven "colors" in which the white light of the Logos is refracted. They are present throughout TCF but are the primary subject of *Esoteric Psychology* I & II.

## The Seven Rays

| Ray | Name | Aspect |
|-----|------|--------|
| I | Will or Power | First Aspect |
| II | Love-Wisdom | Second Aspect |
| III | Active Intelligence | Third Aspect |
| IV | Harmony through Conflict | Attribute |
| V | Concrete Knowledge or Science | Attribute |
| VI | Devotion or Idealism | Attribute |
| VII | Ceremonial Order or Magic | Attribute |

Rays I–III are the rays of aspect (the [[the-three-fires]] in qualitative form). Rays IV–VII are rays of attribute, sub-differentiations of the third aspect.

## Rays and the Logos

Each of the seven sacred planets embodies one of the seven rays. [[The-logos|The Solar Logos]] itself is on the second Ray of Love-Wisdom — this is said to be the dominant quality of the present solar system.

## Rays and the Human Being

Every person has multiple ray influences operating simultaneously:
- [[monad|Monadic ray]] — fixed for an entire solar system period
- [[egoic-ray|Soul ray]] — fixed for a long cycle of incarnations
- [[personality-ray|Personality ray]] — changes each life
- Rays of the mental, astral, and physical bodies

The interaction of these rays produces the quality of a life and its karma.

## Rays and the Three Fires

The rays qualify *how* each fire expresses — they are the differentiation within unity. The seven rays of a solar system are the seven ways in which the Logos's triple fire manifests.
