# Personality Ray

The ray governing the personality as a whole — the integrated lower self. Division B of Section One in TCF treats the personality ray in relation to [[fire-by-friction]].

## Nature

- Changes with each incarnation, unlike the [[egoic-ray]] which is stable
- Governs the overall quality of the personality vehicle in a given life
- Determines the line of least resistance and the characteristic strengths and weaknesses
- One of the seven [[the-rays|rays]]

## Rays of the Sub-vehicles

The personality has its own ray, but each of its vehicles also has a ray:
- Personality ray (overall)
- Mental body ray
- Astral body ray
- Physical body ray

The interaction of these four ray influences in a given life creates the psychological and energetic signature of that incarnation.

## Relationship to [[Fire-by-Friction]]

The personality ray expresses through [[fire-by-friction]] — the fire of the lower bodies. It governs how that fire is qualified and directed. A Ray I personality will express the third fire through will and drive; a Ray II through sensitivity; a Ray VII through order and ritual, and so on.

## The Task of Alignment

The purpose of incarnation is progressively to bring the personality ray into alignment with the [[egoic-ray]]. When this is achieved, the personality becomes a transparent vehicle for soul quality — and the path to the third [[initiation]] opens.
