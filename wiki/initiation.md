# Initiation

The graduated expansion of consciousness through which the three fires are progressively integrated and the human being moves from identification with the personality to identification with the soul and eventually with spirit. TCF treats initiation as the structural purpose of the entire system.

## The Nine Initiations

TCF and *Initiation, Human and Solar* describe five human-kingdom initiations. [[Rays-and-initiations|*The Rays and the Initiations*]] extends the arc to nine, covering the post-human planetary and cosmic degrees.

### The Five Human-Kingdom Initiations

| Initiation | Name | Key Event | Fire |
|------------|------|-----------|------|
| 1st | Birth | Soul takes control of the physical; [[kundalini]] begins to move | [[fire-by-friction]] partially mastered |
| 2nd | Baptism | Soul takes control of the astral; desire purified | [[fire-by-friction]] of astral plane subdued |
| 3rd | Transfiguration | Soul takes control of the mind; first direct contact with the [[monad]] | [[solar-fire]] at its height; [[electric-fire]] first contacted |
| 4th | Crucifixion / Renunciation | Causal body destroyed; soul and personality merge | [[solar-fire]] consumes its own vehicle |
| 5th | Revelation | Liberation from the human kingdom; the Adept | [[electric-fire]] increasingly operative |

### The Four Planetary / Cosmic Initiations (R&I contribution)

| Initiation | Name | Key Event |
|------------|------|-----------|
| 6th | Decision | The Master chooses one of seven Paths beyond the human scheme |
| 7th | Resurrection | Full monadic expression; liberation from planetary life |
| 8th | Transition | Beyond planetary scheme; cosmic astral plane transcended |
| 9th | Refusal | The Great Renunciation at the cosmic level |

The 6th through 9th initiations are treated only briefly — they lie beyond the range of the concrete mind to grasp, mirroring TCF's explicit statement about [[electric-fire]]. R&I describes them primarily in terms of what is *left behind* rather than what is entered.

## The Fires and Initiation

Each initiation is characterized by a specific interplay of the three fires:
- The fires of [[fire-by-friction]] are progressively stilled and aligned
- [[Solar-fire]] unfolds the petals of the egoic lotus
- [[Electric-fire]] begins to be contacted at the third initiation

## The Kundalini Connection

[[Kundalini]] rising through the spinal triangles is the physiological signature of initiatory progress. The fire, which has been latent, is awakened and directed upward by the growing influence of [[solar-fire]] (soul).

## Initiation at the Planetary and Cosmic Scale

[[The-logos|The Planetary Logos]] is itself an initiate. The Earth's spiritual history is partly the story of its own Logos taking its next cosmic initiation. Human initiation is nested within — and participates in — this larger process.

## The Hierarchy

The initiatory process is overseen by the Masters — beings who have already completed the human kingdom initiations and are now taking cosmic initiations. The Buddha, the Christ, and other figures in the hierarchy are located within this map.
