# Solar Fire

The second of [[the-three-fires]]. Corresponds to Soul, the Second Aspect, Love-Wisdom. Its instrument is [[manas]] — mind. Section Two of TCF, the longest section, is devoted entirely to it.

## Core Characteristics

- The mediating fire — stands between Electric Fire (spirit) and Fire by Friction (matter)
- The fire of mind; the substance of the causal body
- Governs the egoic lotus (the soul vehicle on the higher mental plane)
- Its law is the [[law-of-attraction]]

## The Structure of Section Two

Bailey divides the treatment of Solar Fire into six divisions:

| Division | Subject |
|----------|---------|
| A | [[manas]] — its nature |
| B | Manas as cosmic, systemic, and human factor |
| C | [[egoic-ray]] and solar fire |
| D | [[thought-elementals]] and [[fire-elementals]] |
| E | [[motion]] on the plane of mind |
| F | [[law-of-attraction]] |

## The Egoic Lotus

The causal body — the soul's vehicle — is described as a lotus of fire, its petals formed of solar fire, its jewel at the centre being a point of electric fire. The unfoldment of the petals is the process of [[initiation]].

## Relationship to Manas

Solar fire and [[manas]] are almost synonymous in TCF — mind is the expression of this fire in manifest form. [[The-logos|The Solar Logos]] is described as a cosmic mental entity.
