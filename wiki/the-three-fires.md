# The Three Fires

The organizing framework of *A Treatise on Cosmic Fire*. All manifest existence is understood as the interplay of three fires, each corresponding to one of the three aspects of the divine trinity.

## The Framework

| Fire | Aspect | Principle | Section |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| [[electric-fire]] | First / Spirit / Will | The Monad | Section Three |
| [[solar-fire]] | Second / Soul / Love-Wisdom | The Ego | Section Two |
| [[fire-by-friction]] | Third / Matter / Active Intelligence | The Personality | Section One |

> "Electric Fire, or Spirit. Solar Fire, or Soul. Fire by Friction, or Body, or Matter."
> — TCF, Introductory Postulates

## The Correspondence

The same triadic structure repeats at every scale — this is the law of [[macrocosm-microcosm]]:

- In the cosmos: the three aspects of the Logos
- In the solar system: will, love, intelligence
- In the human being: [[monad]], ego (soul), personality
- In the atom: positive nucleus, electrons, atomic motion

## Notes on the Limitations

Bailey explicitly states that [[electric-fire]] (Section Three) cannot be fully expounded — it is "devoid of form and therefore incognisable by the lower concrete mind." Only initiates of the third degree and above can consciously grasp it. The bulk of the Treatise therefore focuses on [[solar-fire]] and [[fire-by-friction]].

## Relationship to the Stanzas of Dzyan

[[Stanzas-of-dzyan]] form the root-text. The three fires are the key with which Bailey reads and elaborates them.
