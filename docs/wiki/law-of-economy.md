# Law of Economy

The first of three cosmic laws treated in TCF. Governs [[fire-by-friction]] — the fire of matter. Division F of Section One is devoted to it.

## Definition

The Law of Economy produces the line of least resistance in matter. It ensures that substance moves along the path requiring the minimum expenditure of force. It is the law of the Third Aspect — Active Intelligence.

## Its Effects in Matter

- Governs the dissociation of atoms
- Underlies the formation and dissolution of all physical structures
- Produces the tendency of matter to return to homogeneity
- Is the basis of entropy as understood physically

## Its Three Expressions

Bailey gives three aspects of the law's operation:
1. **In matter**: dissociation and atomic activity
2. **In form**: the drive toward equilibrium and rest
3. **In mind**: the tendency of the mind toward the line of least resistance (habit, rut, convention)

## Relationship to the Other Laws

The three cosmic laws correspond to the three fires and the three aspects:

| Law | Fire | Aspect |
|-----|------|--------|
| Law of Economy | [[fire-by-friction]] | Third / Matter |
| [[law-of-attraction]] | [[solar-fire]] | Second / Soul |
| Law of Synthesis | [[electric-fire]] | First / Spirit |

The Law of Synthesis (governing Electric Fire) is barely touched on in TCF — it operates above the level accessible to the concrete mind.
