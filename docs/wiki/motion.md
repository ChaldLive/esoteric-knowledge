# Motion

One of the primary characteristics of fire in TCF — all fire is motion; all motion is fire. Division E of Section One covers motion on the physical and astral planes; Division E of Section Two covers motion on the plane of mind.

## The Threefold Goal and Function

On the physical and astral planes, motion is described as having a threefold goal and a threefold function — expressing the three aspects through movement itself.

## Types of Motion

TCF identifies several modes of motion corresponding to the planes and fires:

| Type | Plane | Fire |
|------|-------|------|
| Rotary | Physical (atomic spin) | [[fire-by-friction]] |
| Spiral-cyclic | Astral and lower mental | [[fire-by-friction]] / transition |
| Onward/forward (progressive) | Higher mental and above | [[solar-fire]] |

The evolution of consciousness is the evolution from rotary through spiral-cyclic to progressive motion.

## Motion and the [[law-of-economy]]

The [[law-of-economy]] manifests as a tendency toward rotary motion — atoms spin. This is the baseline activity of matter, self-contained and returning on itself.

## Motion and [[solar-fire]]

[[solar-fire]] introduces spiral-cyclic motion — evolution, the ascending spiral. The same circuit is traversed but at a higher level each time. This is the signature of soul-guided development.

## Motion at Cosmic Scale

At the cosmic scale, the solar system itself moves through space — both in rotation and in progressive movement through the galaxy. These motions are treated as expressions of the three cosmic fires at the macrocosmic level.
