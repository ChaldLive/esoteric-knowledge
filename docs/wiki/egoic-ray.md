# Egoic Ray

The ray of the soul — the qualitative color of a person's soul-life, fixed for a long cycle of incarnations. Division C of Section Two in TCF treats the egoic ray in relation to [[solar-fire]].

## Nature

- One of the seven [[the-rays|rays]], expressing through the causal body (egoic lotus)
- Determines the primary quality of the soul's expression across lives
- Remains constant through many incarnations while the [[personality-ray]] changes each life
- Determined by the ray of the Planetary Logos whose scheme the soul is evolving within

## The Egoic Lotus

The soul's vehicle is described as a lotus of [[solar-fire]] on the higher mental plane. Its petals unfold progressively through [[initiation]]:
- **Knowledge petals** (outermost): link intellect with soul awareness
- **Love petals** (middle): link love-aspiration with soul
- **Sacrifice petals** (innermost): link will with atmic purpose
- **The Jewel**: at the centre — a point of [[electric-fire]], the [[monad]]'s touch

The ray of the soul gives the quality (color, fragrance) of the entire lotus.

## Relationship to the Personality Ray

The [[personality-ray]] and the egoic ray must eventually come into alignment — the personality fully expressing the soul's quality. This alignment is the work of the later stages of the path before the third [[initiation]].
