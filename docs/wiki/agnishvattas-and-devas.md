# Agnishvattas and the Devas

How the Solar Angels relate to the wider [[deva-evolution]]. The short answer from TCF: the [[agnishvattas]] *are* devas. They are the highest order of the builders, and the one order that has already passed through the human stage. That double status is what lets them stand between the two parallel evolutions.

## The Agnishvattas are devas

TCF names them plainly as devas, several times:

- "the Agnishvattas, or the Fire devas of the mental plane" (TCF, l. 23021)
- "the Pitris, the builders of the body of the Ego... the Agnishvattas, the great devas of Mind" (l. 23314–23315)
- In the table of correspondences: "The 5th group of Devas / Fire Devas of the mental plane" (l. 20439–20441)
- Their alternative name, **Manasadevas** ("mind-devas"), is used throughout Division B

They also belong to the top grade of builder. "The greater Builders are the solar Pitris, whilst the lesser Builders are the lunar ancestors" (l. 20750), and "a solar Logos works only through the greater Builders, the Manasaputras" (l. 19163). Within the deva hierarchy described in [[deva-evolution]], the Agnishvattas are therefore on the **evolutionary, positive, conscious** side.

## What sets them apart: they have been human

TCF draws the line between solar and lunar Pitris on one point:

> "those lunar Pitris... are deva groups but (unlike the Agnishvattas) they have not passed through the human stage; for them it has yet to be achieved." (l. 26186–26188)

The principle behind it: "any life can only give out that which it has possessed" (l. 26190–26196). Hence:

> "The solar Pitris, therefore, could give to man his consciousness; the lunar Pitris could give to him the instinctual consciousness of his vehicles." (l. 26197–26198)

The Agnishvattas reached self-consciousness in a previous system (see [[agnishvattas]]), so they can pass it on. The lunar Pitris have not, so they can only give form-consciousness.

| Order | Fire devas of the mental plane | Deva groups of the three lower sheaths |

## Built of their own substance

The causal body is made of deva substance. Bailey speaks of "self-consciousness, as it is produced through the medium of the particular type of deva substance which the Agnishvattas provided for the body of the Ego" (l. 24663–24665). These Manasadevas "are indeed the energy and substance of the cosmic Heavenly Man" (l. 24668–24669).

This fits the general statement in [[deva-evolution]] that "the sumtotal of manas is pure deva essence" (l. 13841). The Agnishvattas are that essence in self-conscious form.

## Solar Lord over lunar lords

Inside each human being the two orders of deva meet as a polarity, and the path is described as the higher one gaining control over the lower:

- "bringing about a domination of the lunar lords through the radiant control of the solar Lord. This is the occult method." (l. 27203–27204)
- "The lunar Lords have had their day; now Agni, as the solar Lord of life and energy, will assume due importance in human life." (l. 20501–20502)
- From the Old Commentary: "The solar Angel must put out the light of the lunar angels and then for lack of warmth and light, that which has served to hinder no longer is." (l. 31607–31608)
- But the lunar side is not simply discarded: "The Lunar Angels have to reach the plane of the Solar Angels" (footnote quoting SD I, 203; l. 21542).

When a form dissolves, "the solar Devas (or radiant energy) return to the central Heart," leaving "the lesser deva substance dependent upon its own internal heat," and "the lesser builders and devas return to their group soul" (l. 27873–27877).

## Why this matters: the hinge between the two evolutions

Put together, the passages suggest the Agnishvattas work as a **hinge**. By nature they are devas, builders and substance. By history they have been human and are self-conscious. That is why they can build a *human* consciousness out of *deva* substance. In the language of [[deva-evolution]], they bridge in matter on behalf of a stream that bridges in essence.

*Librarian's flag:* TCF does not use the word "hinge" or state this synthesis outright. It is an inference from the passages above, offered as a reading, not a citation.

## Open questions

- If the Agnishvattas have already been through the human stage, do the lunar Pitris now face a comparable passage, with humanity in the role the Agnishvattas once played? The SD line "the Lunar Angels have to reach the plane of the Solar Angels" points that way. [[agnishvattas]] notes that in the next system the Agnishvattas themselves will be "below the threshold", as the lunar Pitris are now.
- How do the Agnishvattas relate to the "solar devas" of Section One (l. 2757, l. 2800), who transmit pranic fluid? It is the same word, but they appear to be a different order. This is not resolved here.
