# Monad

The "Father in Heaven" — the spark of pure spirit in the human being. The first aspect, the vehicle of [[electric-fire]] in the individual. Largely beyond elaboration in TCF for the same reason Electric Fire itself resists exposition.

## Nature

- A point of electric fire; a fragment of the Solar Logos's own first-aspect life
- Situated on the monadic plane (second cosmic plane; second of the seven solar planes)
- Not to be confused with the soul (which is on the higher mental / causal plane) — the monad is several planes above

## The Trinity in the Human Being

| Fire | Aspect | Vehicle | Plane |
|------|--------|---------|-------|
| [[electric-fire]] | Spirit | Monad | Monadic plane |
| [[solar-fire]] | Soul | Causal body / egoic lotus | Higher mental |
| [[fire-by-friction]] | Personality | Lower bodies | Lower mental, astral, physical |

## The Monad and Initiation

Conscious contact with the monad is not achieved until the third [[initiation]] — the Transfiguration. It is the goal toward which soul development is working. Below that level, the disciple works with the soul; the monad remains the distant "Father."

## The Jewel in the Lotus

At the heart of the egoic lotus (the soul's causal body) there is said to be a jewel — a point of electric fire. This is the monad's reflection into the soul vehicle; the closest the personality-soul complex comes to direct contact with its source.
