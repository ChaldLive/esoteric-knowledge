# Manas

The mind principle. The instrumental expression of [[solar-fire]] in manifest form. Division B of Section Two — by far the longest single section in TCF — is devoted to manas as a cosmic, systemic, and human factor.

## Three Scales of Operation

Bailey consistently treats manas at three levels simultaneously:

| Scale | Expression |
|-------|-----------|
| Cosmic | Mind of the Solar Logos; the cosmic mental plane |
| Systemic | The mental plane of our solar system; planetary mind |
| Human | The mind principle in the individual — lower and higher |

## The Manasaputras

The "Sons of Mind" — the Agnishvattas — are the entities who implanted the spark of mind in humanity in the third root race. This is the esoteric account of the emergence of self-consciousness. It connects to the Promethean myth and to H.P. Blavatsky's account in *The Secret Doctrine*.

## Lower and Higher Manas

- **Lower manas**: the concrete mind, associated with the fifth sub-plane of the mental plane; the instrument of analysis, science, and separative thought
- **Higher manas**: abstract mind, associated with the causal body; the vehicle of intuition and synthesis; directly linked to [[solar-fire]]

The bridge between them is the [[antahkarana]] — a thread of fire built consciously by the disciple.

## Manas and the Egoic Lotus

The petals of the egoic lotus are organized by manas: knowledge petals, love petals, sacrifice petals. Their unfoldment is the story of [[initiation]].

## Karma and Manas

Manas is the recording faculty — it registers cause and effect across lives. The Lipika Lords (Lords of Karma) work through the manasic principle.
