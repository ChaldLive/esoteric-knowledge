# Discipleship

The graduated relationship between an aspirant and a Master of the Wisdom, moving from indirect
contact through a chela on the physical plane to constant, at-will rapport. *Discipleship in the
New Age* treats discipleship as a technical status with named stages, distinct from — but woven
through — the ladder of [[initiation]].

## The Six Stages of Discipleship

Given in DINA Vol. I, Section Three. These track the disciple's *mode of contact with the Master*,
not initiatory rank directly, though the two scales are correlated:

1. **Little Chelaship** — contact only through another chela on the physical plane.
2. **Chela in the Light** — directed by a higher disciple from egoic (soul) level.
3. **Accepted Discipleship** — direct contact via dream, symbol, thoughtform, meditation, or a
   remembered interview in the Ashram.
4. **Chela on the Thread** — can draw the Master's instantaneous attention in emergency (the
   sutratma stage).
5. **Chela within the Aura** — knows the method of calling an interview at will.
6. **Chela within the Master's Heart** — constant rapport; immediate preparation for, or
   specialised work following, initiation.

A seventh, unnamed stage — "a blending of the Lights" — is mentioned but left untranslated.

## Correlation with Initiation

The Tibetan states the stages are "all of them related to one or other of the initiations," but
the mapping is not one stage per initiation:

- **Little Chelaship** is tied directly to the **1st initiation**.
- **Accepted Discipleship** (stage III) opens after the 1st initiation and spans **both the 1st and
  2nd**; a disciple who takes the 3rd initiation is technically no longer an "accepted disciple,"
  though he remains in the Master's group.
- **Chela within the Master's Heart** (stage VI) opens beyond the **4th initiation**: the disciple
  passes out of a Master's supervision into direct relation with the Christ, and the stage is
  described as entered "through the open door of Nirvana" — the threshold of the 5th initiation in
  [[rays-and-initiations]] terms.

The middle stages (Chela on the Thread, Chela within the Aura) are not given an explicit numeric
anchor; they fall thematically between the 2nd and 4th initiations. Full correlation, with sourcing
for each claim, in [Six Stages of Discipleship and Their Correlation with Initiation](../outputs/six-stages-of-discipleship-and-initiation.md).

## A Second Schema: Status Within the Ashram

DINA Vol. II gives a separate six-stage sequence describing the disciple's *status and duty within
the Ashram* (beginning with "the awakening urge"), explicitly distinguished by the Tibetan from the
contact-stages above. Not yet correlated with initiation in these notes — a thread to develop.

## Service's Changing Character Across the Stages

Service is named, alongside purification and devotion, as part of the exoteric foundation expected
of *all* true aspirants — present from before Little Chelaship. But its quality shifts with the
stages above: effortful and self-conscious in the aspirant; impersonal and Ashram-directed in
Accepted Discipleship; and, by the Chela within the Master's Heart, no longer an act of will at all
but pure radiation — "a radiation which evokes activity in others" (DINA I). *Discipleship in the
New Age* Vol. II calls Service, not Meditation, the true engine driving this whole progression
toward the 4th initiation. Full treatment: [The Science of Meditation and the Science of Service,
Correlated with Initiation](../outputs/meditation-and-service-and-initiation.md).
