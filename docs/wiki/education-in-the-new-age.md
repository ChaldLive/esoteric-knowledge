# Education in the New Age

Alice Bailey, 1954. A comparatively short volume, but structurally significant: its own synopsis
states that its three sections form a single arc that continues directly into *[[rays-and-initiations|A
Treatise on the Seven Rays, Vol. V]]* — this book lays the foundation and develops Section One in
full; R&I supplies the advanced teaching for Section Two (the Antahkarana); Section Three (the
Science of Service) is only briefly defined here, in Chapter III, and is not elaborated at length
anywhere in the corpus the way the Antahkarana is.

## Structure

| Chapter | Title |
|---|---|
| I | The Objective of the New Education |
| II | The Cultural Unfoldment of the Race |
| III | The Next Step in the Mental Development of Humanity |
| IV | The Culture of the Individual |
| V | The Science of the Antahkarana |

The book's own synopsis (given in three sections, not matching the chapter numbers above) makes the
intended arc explicit:

- **Section One: The Objectives of the Future Education** — developed in this book. Cultural
  unfoldment of the race, the next step in humanity's mental development, and the culture of the
  individual as citizen of two worlds, parent, and integrated personality.
- **Section Two: The Antahkarana** — the nature of the bridge, the technique of its construction,
  and the ray methods used. Bailey states this section "appears in *A Treatise on the Seven Rays,
  Vol. V*" — i.e., in [[rays-and-initiations]]. This book's Chapter V is the seed of that later,
  fuller teaching.
- **Section Three: The Three Major Sciences of the Aquarian Age** — the Science of the Antahkarana,
  the Science of Meditation, and the Science of Service, as a unified curriculum for the coming age.

## Why It Matters for This Library

Chapter V, "The Science of the Antahkarana," predates and directly seeds the [[antahkarana]] teaching
elaborated at length in R&I. It introduces the same core distinction between **energy** (sutratma —
what flows into the individual) and **force** (what is projected outward after concentration), and
frames antahkarana-building explicitly as an educational — not only initiatory — discipline: the
"New Education" is, in essence, training in bridge-building from childhood onward, rather than a
technique reserved for advanced disciples.

This reframes [[antahkarana]] slightly: R&I presents the science from the standpoint of the disciple
already on the Path: EITN presents the same science as the destination toward which *all* future
education should orient, for every child, well before formal discipleship begins.

## The Three Sciences, Defined

Chapter III gives crisp working definitions of all three sciences named in the synopsis:

- **The Science of the Antahkarana** — "the new and true science of the mind," building the bridge
  between personality and soul, then soul and Spiritual Triad, in the substance of the three higher
  mental levels.
- **The Science of Meditation** — explicitly a "subsidiary branch, preparatory to" the Antahkarana
  science: it builds the bridge's first half, in mental substance, and (in an unnamed extension
  Bailey calls "the science of vision") its second half, in light substance.
- **The Science of Service** — presented as growing "normally and naturally" out of the other two
  succeeding; "the true science of creation," establishing continuity between soul purpose and
  outer act.

Correlated with the initiation ladder — including a stronger, competing statement from
*Discipleship in the New Age* Vol. II that ranks Service *above* Meditation and ties it directly to
the 4th initiation — in [The Science of Meditation and the Science of Service, Correlated with
Initiation](../outputs/meditation-and-service-and-initiation.md).
