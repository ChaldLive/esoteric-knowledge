# Agnishvattas

The Solar Angels. The Fire Devas of the mental plane. The most significant class of beings in relation to human evolution, constituting the subject of the longest sustained treatment in *A Treatise on Cosmic Fire* (Division B of Section Two). Bailey describes them as "the most stupendous subject in connection with our planetary evolution."

Also named: Manasadevas, Solar Pitris, Fire Dhyanis, Lords of the Flame, Manasaputras (Sons of Mind), the Asuras.

---

## Nature and Origin

The Agnishvattas are **Nirvanis from a preceding Mahamanvantara** (SD II, 83–84). In the solar system prior to ours, they achieved self-consciousness through material evolution. The quality they carry from that experience is *active intelligence*. At the close of that system they entered a nirvanic pralaya and emerged into this one when conditions permitted.

Their substance is energised by the principle of **ahamkara** — "I-ness," the sense of separate selfhood. This is not incidental: they *are* the I-principle in concentrated form. "In time and space, and on the mental plane, they are Man himself in essential essence" (TCF, ~23775). They embody the fifth Hierarchy and are the producers of self-consciousness and the builders of man's body of realisation.

They are described as the "Heart of the Dhyan-chohanic body" (SD II, 96), their energy emanating from the **spiritual sun** (as opposed to prana, which comes from the physical sun). They constitute a peculiar type of electrical force — the "transmuting fires of the system" — passing the life of God through their bodies of flame as it moves in both directions across the spirit–matter threshold.

---

## The Reason for Their Arrival

Their descent was governed by two converging necessities:

1. **Readiness below.** Individualisation becomes possible only when the heart centre of a Heavenly Man attains a specific vibratory capacity. For the Earth scheme this corresponded to the middle of the third root race (Lemurian), fourth round, third chain.

2. **Cosmic trigger from above.** A WORD sounded forth producing desire in the Triad for self-expression. The sound of the lower manifestation blended with it and "rose up into the Heavens," producing a response in three allied constellations — Sirius and two of the Pleiades. This released an energy that swept into the solar system, "carrying with it those solar angels who rested in the Heart of God until the hour was come" (TCF, ~23742).

The Agnishvattas entered the scheme "on a wave of manasic energy from the head centre of the Logos." Passing through His Heart centre, they became differentiated into seven groups, directed themselves as streams of energy to particular schemes, and their contact with any scheme is what produces the manifestation of the fourth Creative Hierarchy — the human kingdom.

---

## Their Evolutionary Opportunity

They sought to add **love-wisdom (buddhi)** to the active intelligence developed in the previous system. This solar system is governed by the vibration of the cosmic astral plane; the lesson available here — unavailable before — is the development of the capacity for love as a cosmic principle.

Their work in this system therefore serves two purposes simultaneously: it creates the human kingdom, and it transforms the Agnishvattas themselves. The nirvanis who emerge from this mahamanvantara will be, in Bailey's phrase, "active intelligent love" — the quality that will characterise the beings who seed the third solar system.

---

## The Effect on the Human Units They Individualised

At the moment of individualisation, the Manasadevas produce four sequential effects on the mental plane:

| Stage | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Formation of the **ninefold egoic lotus** on the third subplane of the mental plane, petals constructed out of the Agnishvattas' own substance and coloured with the fire of [[manas]] |
| 2 | Appearance of a **manasic triangle** circulating between the manasic permanent atom, the centre of the lotus, and the mental unit — the nucleus of the [[antahkarana]] |
| 3 | A **downflow of buddhi** along the triangle, producing three innermost petals that close over the central flame (the "jewel in the Lotus," not revealed until high initiation). The egoic lotus is now twelve petals; the three permanent atoms enclosed within it |
| 4 | The triangle is withdrawn to the centre; energy centralises to drive the first downflow into the three worlds |

The causal body at this point is embryonic — "white," transparent, in equilibrium. It "remains with the new-born ego first to upset the equilibrium, and then to regain it, at the close of the process, producing a radiant form, full of primal colours" (TCF, ~23922).

Long-term, the Agnishvattas work through the mental units of individual Egos, impressing logoic purpose — first received unconsciously, later cooperated with consciously. After the third initiation, the will or purpose aspect predominates.

**The positive force of the Manasadevas produces initiation.** The Hierophant at each initiation transmits higher manasic energy via the Rod of Power, causing successive petals to unfold until the electric fire of spirit stands revealed at the lotus heart. Through their agency, man can ultimately "become conscious without the manasic vehicle" — the Agnishvattas enable the transcendence of the very principle they embody.

## The Three Groups and Their Work

| Group | Centre | Work |
|-------|--------|------|
| Highest | Logoic head centre | Works through manasic permanent atoms; embodies the will-to-be in dense physical incarnation |
| Middle | Logoic heart centre | Connected with causal bodies of all Egos; the prime group of this solar system |
| Third | Logoic throat centre | Works through mental units on the fourth subplane; governs the Ego's capacity to see, hear, and sound in the occult sense |

The threefold division among the Agnishvattas who worked with Earth at individualisation also resolves into three (or five, with subdivisions): those who refused to incarnate, those who implanted the manasic spark, and those who took bodies and moulded the type.

---

## Their Destiny

**Between rounds and schemes:** During pralaya, the Agnishvattas withdraw from manifestation and return to "a cosmic centre of which the logoic head centre is but a dim reflection — they return the richer for experience" (TCF, ~25015).

**In the fifth round:** At its middle point, the fifth Hierarchy rises to full power. This precipitates the final racial Armageddon — the planetary Kurukshetra — a crisis in which the manasic vehicle rebels against the translation of buddhi. It is succeeded by a Judgment Day in which those in whom manas is over-potent or under-developed are held over, while those living the buddhic life continue. The "Dragons of Wisdom" then rule.

**In the next solar system:** The Agnishvattas will occupy a position analogous to that of the [[lunar-pitris]] now — part of the divine consciousness but "below the threshold" of what evolved beings of that system aspire to. Humanity in that future system will "mount upward by means of the serpent of wisdom," and by:

> *"the domination and control of the Agnishvattas, achieve something of which even the illuminated mind of the highest Dhyan-chohan cannot yet conceive."* (TCF, ~23309)

**What cannot be stated:** The full mystery of the Manasaputras is concealed within their connection to the fifth Hierarchy and the secret of buddhi — the sixth or Christ principle. This "cannot be mentioned outside initiated circles. It holds hid the possibility of egoic unfoldment, and keeps secret the karma of the Heavenly Men, the five [[kumaras|Kumaras]]" (TCF, ~23799–23802). Bailey states explicitly that further revelation on cosmic manas cannot be given at this time.

**The Kumara connection made explicit:** the Lord of Venus — one of the five [[kumaras]] — is stated to embody in Himself the function of the fifth Hierarchy of Agnishvattas (TCF, ~25303–25310). The Kumaras are this same manasic principle concentrated at planetary scale; the Agnishvattas are it diffused across every incarnating human unit. See [[kumaras]] for the concentrated counterpart.
