# Kundalini

The latent fire coiled at the base of the spine; the concentrated, sleeping form of [[fire-by-friction]] in the human system. Division D of Section One in TCF is devoted to kundalini and the spine.

## Nature

- Not identical to [[prana]] — prana is received from without; kundalini is the fire latent *within* matter itself
- Its arousal is the physiological correlate of spiritual initiation
- Works through a system of three triangles (spinal centers and head centers)

## The Three Triangles

TCF describes three triangles through which kundalini works:

1. **In the head** — crown, ajna, alta major centers
2. **In the body** — heart, throat, and one other center
3. **At the base of the spine** — the base triangle from which the fire rises

The specific triangles are deliberately left incomplete in the public text. Bailey is explicit that full instruction belongs to initiatory transmission, not published teaching.

## Kundalini and Initiation

The rising of kundalini through the spinal channel is directly linked to the initiatory process. At each [[initiation]], a triangle is completed and kundalini reaches a new height. The process is not separable from the simultaneous unfoldment of the egoic lotus ([[manas]], [[solar-fire]]).

## Warning on Premature Arousal

TCF is explicit and repeated in warning against deliberate arousal of kundalini outside of the natural process of spiritual development. The fire, aroused prematurely, destroys rather than illuminates. Bailey distances this teaching from certain Tantric and Hatha Yoga practices.

## Kundalini at the Planetary Scale

The [[macrocosm-microcosm]] principle applies: the planet has its own kundalini — a fire at the center of the Earth — and so does the solar system.
