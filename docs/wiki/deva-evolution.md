# The Deva Evolution

The angelic or builder stream: the life *within* substance, evolving alongside humanity as its parallel and complement. TCF insists on parity: "the deva evolution is of equal importance to that of the human" (TCF, l. 30245).

> "Man bridges in essence; the devas bridge in matter." (TCF, l. 10472)

## Purpose: the builders of form

- The devas are the form-building, substance aspect of divinity. TCF calls them "the feminine aspect of the divine hermaphroditic Man" (l. 19936), working with the third aspect, [[fire-by-friction]].
- They are also the substance of mind: "the devas are the personification of the active universal mind" (l. 10471), and "the sumtotal of manas is pure deva essence" (l. 13841). See [[manas]].
- With man, the devas "form the SOUL of a Heavenly Man", as distinct from the lesser builders, who form His body (l. 10468). See [[the-logos]].

## Two grades of builder

TCF divides the deva and elemental lives into two arcs (l. 20713–20722):

| | Greater Builders (devas proper) | Lesser Builders (elementals) |
|---|---|---|
| Arc | Evolutionary | Involutionary |
| Polarity | Positive force | Negative force |
| Mode | Conscious workers | Blind workers |

The lesser builders include the [[fire-elementals]] and the builders of [[thought-elementals]]. On the physical plane the whole hierarchy is ruled by the deva Kshiti, "of rank and power equal to a Chohan of a Ray", with a council of seven devas "who handle all that concerns the deva evolution" (l. 30247–30252).

## Method: the contrast with man

TCF sets the two evolutions side by side (l. 22621–22642):

| | Man | Deva |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrates | The *aspects* of divinity | The *attributes* of divinity |
| Must learn | To see (inner vision) | To hear (inner hearing) |
| Evolves by | Contact and experience; "He expands" | "The lessening of contact. Limitation is the law for them" |
| Development | Self-control | "Being controlled" |
| Innate quality | Love, the force producing coherency | Intelligence, the force producing activity |
| Will appears as | Self-consciousness | Constructive vibration |

The great devas express ideas through "colour which can be heard, and sound which can be seen. Man reverses the process" (l. 22612). Symbols are a shared language between the two streams because they "can be comprehended alike by the evolved of both evolutions" (l. 22614).

Each deva group has its own path of attainment. For example, "for the violet devas the path of attainment lies through feeling", and the green devas serve through magnetisation (l. 30310–30314). The guardian angels of the race are drawn from the white devas of air and water (l. 30302–30304).

## Aim: convergence

The two streams evolve "through interaction with each other" (l. 22608), touch at fixed points, and merge in the end:

1. **The vegetable kingdom:** "one of the first and temporary approximations between the evolving human Monad, and the evolving deva Monad" (l. 19931).
2. **The [[the-planes|buddhic plane]]:** "the two great evolutions (human and deva) find their group unity on the buddhic plane, and portions of both hierarchies blend and merge so as to form the body of the divine Hermaphrodite" (l. 11384).
3. **The second (monadic) plane:** "a final merging" (l. 19933).
4. **The seventh round:** "will mark the merging of the two evolutions, the human and the deva" (l. 20188).

### Shared karma on the buddhic plane

The meeting is also karmic. "The fourth plane is the plane of at-one-ment for certain deva and human units, and certain groups (the fourth Creative Hierarchy and the sixth Deva Hierarchy) have a pronounced karma to work out together" (l. 14722–14724). The same passage says that "in the sixth subrace the deva evolution will be exceedingly prominent" (l. 14720). Elsewhere man is the mediator of karma within the deva world: the "karmic enmity existing between the two great groups of devas... can only be united by the mediator, man" (l. 33890–33891).

In the Heavenly Man the two "are equally blended, and in the course of the mahamanvantara the imperfect Gods become perfect" (l. 22644).

## A caution for the human stream

Man's task runs through consciousness, not form. Where the approach is "centred on the form and not on the Spirit, the tendency is to deva worship, deva contact and black magic" (l. 16726). The deva side is cooperated with, not pursued. See [[antahkarana]].

## Fit with this repo's posture

This is complementarity in a pure form: form and life, matter and essence, two evolutions that are each incomplete and that make up one being only when joined. Neither stream is reduced to the other.

## Open questions

- TCF says little about how an individual deva moves from the involutionary to the evolutionary arc. This note does not fill that gap.
- The relation between the devas and the [[agnishvattas]] (themselves called "solar devas" in places) needs its own treatment.
