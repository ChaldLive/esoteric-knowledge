# Thought Elementals

The living entities that constitute and animate thought-forms. Division D of Section Two in TCF treats thought elementals and [[fire-elementals]] together as the builders operating on their respective planes.

## Nature

- Every thought creates a form in mental substance
- That form is animated by an elemental life — a being whose entire existence is coextensive with the thought-form
- When the thought is withdrawn, the elemental is freed and the form dissipates

## The Builders and the Thinker

Bailey distinguishes between:
- **The thinker** (the human being as soul) who provides the impulse and the quality
- **The elemental builders** who respond to that impulse and construct the form in mental matter

The quality of thought — its purity, coherence, and motivation — determines the quality of the elemental attracted to build it.

## Relationship to [[manas]]

Thought elementals are the natural kingdom corresponding to the mental plane, as physical elementals are to the physical. [[manas]] — the mind principle — is their element; [[solar-fire]] their animating energy.

## Dangers

TCF gives careful treatment to the dangers of:
- Negative thought-forms accumulating and taking on independent life
- Thought-forms persisting beyond their useful life and creating obsession or delusion
- Mass thought-forms (egregores) built by groups or nations
