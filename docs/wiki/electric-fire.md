# Electric Fire

The first of [[the-three-fires]]. Corresponds to Spirit, the First Aspect, Will. Its vehicle in the human being is the [[monad]].

## Core Characteristics

- Pure, undifferentiated will
- Pre-formal — has no form and is therefore not accessible to the lower concrete mind
- Associated with the positive pole in all atomic structure
- Its law is not given a name in TCF equivalent to the other two; it transcends formulation

## Limits of Exposition

Bailey opens Section Three with an explicit caution: this fire "will be quite impossible to impart information of a
definite character." It is only accessible to initiates of the third degree and above, who have been "put in conscious
contact with their Father in Heaven, the Monad."

The Nicodemus passage from John's Gospel is cited: _"The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof but canst not tell whence it cometh nor whither it goeth."_

## Relationship to the Other Fires

Electric Fire is the animating source that [[solar-fire]] (mind/soul) and [[fire-by-friction]] (matter) both serve and ultimately return to. Its correct action through the lower fires produces [[initiation]].
