# The Planes

The seven planes of manifestation form the vertical axis of Bailey's cosmology, inherited from Blavatsky's *The Secret Doctrine*. Each plane is a level of vibratory density; each has seven sub-planes.

## The Seven Planes (Solar System Scale)

| Number | Name | Also Called |
|--------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Adi (Divine) | Logoic plane |
| 2 | Anupadaka (Monadic) | Paranirvanic plane |
| 3 | Atmic | Nirvanic plane |
| 4 | Buddhic | Intuitional plane |
| 5 | Mental | Manasic plane |
| 6 | Astral | Emotional / Kamic plane |
| 7 | Physical | Including etheric sub-planes |

## The Physical Plane

Has seven sub-planes, the four highest being the etheric sub-planes. The dense physical (solid, liquid, gas) occupies only the three lowest. [[etheric-body|The etheric body]] is the vehicle on the higher sub-planes of the physical plane.

## The Planes and the Three Fires

- The lower three planes (physical, astral, mental-lower) are the domain of [[fire-by-friction]]
- The higher mental and buddhic are the domain of [[solar-fire]]
- The atmic, monadic, and logoic planes are the domain of [[electric-fire]]

## The Planes and the Logos

From [[the-logos|the Solar Logos]]'s perspective, our seven planes are its seven principles. The entire system is nested — what is the physical plane from one perspective is a sub-plane from a higher perspective.

## Correspondences

The [[macrocosm-microcosm]] principle means the same seven-plane structure exists:
- In the solar system (as above)
- In the planet
- In the human being (as the seven bodies / vehicles)
