# Etheric Body

The vital body; the vehicle that underlies and sustains the dense physical form. Occupies the four higher sub-planes of the physical plane. Division C of Section One in TCF is devoted to the etheric body and [[prana]].

## Nature and Function

- The true physical body, from an esoteric standpoint; the dense physical is its shadow
- Receives, assimilates, and distributes [[prana]] — the vital life energy
- Contains the chakras (force centers) through which energy from higher planes is stepped down
- Is the vehicle through which [[kundalini]] moves once aroused

## Structure

The etheric body is a web of nadis — fine threads of energy — forming a luminous network that interpenetrates and extends slightly beyond the dense physical. The chakras are organized vortices within this web.

## Etheric Body and the Three Fires

- Its substance is part of the domain of [[fire-by-friction]]
- [[prana]] animating it is a form of solar fire at the physical level
- The chakras, when fully activated, express [[electric-fire]] through the form

## Planetary and Solar Etheric Bodies

The [[macrocosm-microcosm]] principle applies: the planet has an etheric body (the etheric planes of Earth), and so does the solar system. [[prana]] flows between them.
