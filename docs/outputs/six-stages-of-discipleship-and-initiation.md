# The Six Stages of Discipleship and Their Correlation with Initiation

*Discipleship in the New Age, Vol. I*, Section Three, gives a six-stage ladder describing not
soul-growth in the abstract but the disciple's *changing mode of rapport with the Master* — how
contact is made, and by what channel. The Tibetan is explicit that this is a deliberately different
lens from the usual literature on discipleship, which focuses on the disciple's character and
effort. Here the focus is what the Master does, and through what medium He can reach the chela.

The six stages, in the Master's own paraphrase (DINA I, Section Three):

1. **Little Chelaship** — the Master is reached only through another chela on the physical plane.
2. **Chela in the Light** — a higher disciple directs the chela from egoic (soul) levels.
3. **Accepted Discipleship** — the Master contacts the chela directly, through a dream, a symbolic
   teaching, a thoughtform, meditation, or a remembered interview in the Ashram.
4. **Chela on the Thread** (the *sutratma* stage) — the chela can, in emergency, draw the Master's
   attention instantaneously.
5. **Chela within the Aura** — the chela knows the method of setting up a call that earns an
   interview at will (within trusted limits).
6. **Chela within the Master's Heart** — constant rapport; the stage of immediate preparation for,
   or specialised work following, initiation.

A seventh, unnamed stage of still closer identification — "a blending of the Lights" — is
mentioned but explicitly left untranslated: "there is no adequate paraphrase of the terms used to
cover the name" (DINA I).

The Tibetan states plainly: *"These stages are all of them related to one or other of the
initiations."* What follows is the correlation the text supports, stage by stage, with the
confidence level of each link flagged.

## The Correlation

| Stage | Name | Initiation correlation | Basis in text |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Little Chelaship | Related to the **1st initiation** | Stated directly: "This one, called Little Chelaship, is related to the first initiation." Also called the "period of the Lemurian consciousness." |
| II | Chela in the Light | The **Atlantean** phase, bridging toward the 1st initiation | "The Atlantean stage of a Chela in the Light, [leads] to the Aryan stage of Accepted Discipleship." Concerned with overcoming glamour on the Probationary Path. |
| III | Accepted Discipleship | Opens *after* the 1st initiation; **spans the 1st and 2nd initiations** | "The term...of Accepted Disciple covers the stages of the first and second initiations; when a disciple has taken the third initiation, he is no longer technically an accepted disciple, even though he still remains in a Master's group." |
| IV | Chela on the Thread | Deepening *within* accepted discipleship, late in the approach to the **3rd initiation** | Requires the chela to have "been an accepted disciple for more than one life." No initiation number is given explicitly — this placement is inferred from sequence and from the stage's demand for long-seasoned decentralisation, the qualities the text elsewhere ties to nearing the 3rd degree. **Inferred, not stated outright.** |
| V | Chela within the Aura | Growing Spiritual Triad contact, culminating **at the 3rd initiation** | The text notes that "after the third initiation, the lower centres have no control whatsoever," and that this stage concerns the disciple's three lower vehicles becoming reflections of "the three aspects of the Spiritual Triad" — the same triad-contact the corpus elsewhere keys to the 3rd degree. **Inferred by convergence of themes, not a direct statement.** |
| VI | Chela within the Master's Heart | **Post-4th initiation**, opening onto the **5th** | Stated directly: at this stage the chela "is no longer...an accepted disciple. He is an initiate of high standing...and has passed out of the supervision...of a Master into direct relation with the Master of all the Masters, the Christ." The text also ties this stage to "the life of the Monad which begins to make itself felt at the third initiation" as an already-active current, and describes entry to the stage as coming "through the open door of Nirvana, the beginning of the Path of the Higher Evolution" — in *[[rays-and-initiations]]* terms, the threshold opened at the 5th initiation (Revelation), where the Path beyond the human kingdom begins. |
| VII (unnamed) | "Blending of the Lights" | Beyond the 5th initiation | No initiation is named; the text withholds even a paraphrase. |

## Reading the Correlation

Two structural points fall out of this:

**The mapping is not one stage per initiation.** Stage III (Accepted Discipleship) alone absorbs
two full initiations. The six stages track *intimacy of contact*, not *initiatory rank* — the two
scales run in parallel but at different rates, and only synchronise cleanly at the seams (1st
initiation opens Stage III; 4th initiation closes it and opens Stage VI). This is consistent with
[[initiation]]'s own point that the technical term "initiate" is reserved by the Hierarchy for the
3rd degree and above — a disciple can be well inside the "Accepted Discipleship" stage of *rapport*
while still, technically, pre-initiate.

**The hinge points are named explicitly; the middle is inferred.** The text is unambiguous about
three joints: Stage I ↔ 1st initiation, the opening/closing brackets of Stage III ↔ 1st/2nd/3rd
initiation, and Stage VI ↔ 4th–5th initiation. Stages IV and V (Chela on the Thread, Chela within
the Aura) sit in the gap between the 2nd and 4th initiations without an explicit numeric anchor;
the placement above follows the text's thematic cues (triad contact, the cessation of lower-centre
control at the 3rd degree) but should be read as a reasoned inference, not a quoted correlation.

## A Second, Distinct Schema (noted, not merged)

*Discipleship in the New Age, Vol. II* (November 1944 instruction) gives a *different* six-stage
sequence — the "awakening urge," etc. — explicitly framed by the Tibetan as a separate axis: not
the disciple's *mode of contact* with the Master, but his *status and duty within the Ashram*. The
two schemas are related but not identical, and Vol. II does not restate or renumber the Vol. I
six stages against initiation; it refers readers back to Vol. I for that correlation. This second
schema is flagged here as a thread for a future note, not folded into the table above.
