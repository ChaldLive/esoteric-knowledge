# Buddhic Symbolic Sensitivity in Humankind

_A synthesis drawn from [[antahkarana]], [[the-planes]], [[manas]], [[education-in-the-new-age]], [[initiation]], and
[[the-rays]]_
_Primary sources: *The Rays and the Initiations* (R&I), *A Treatise on Cosmic Fire* (TCF), *Education in the New Age*
(EITN), *Discipleship in the New Age* Vols. I–II (DINA)_

---

## A note on the term

"Buddhic symbolic sensitivity" is not a single doctrine with its own chapter anywhere in the corpus. It is a *composite*
of the following, and this summary assembles it from three places:

- The phrase itself — "a pictorial and symbolic sensitivity" — appears once in R&I, in the discussion of how the
  antahkarana is built.
- The buddhic plane and the faculty of intuition are treated throughout TCF and DINA.
- What is said about humanity at large (where the race stands, where it is heading) comes mostly from EITN, DINA II and
  a few passages in TCF.

Where the sources disagree or are silent, that is flagged below. The reading of the term as *"the human capacity to
receive buddhic impression and register it through symbol"* is a librarian's working definition, not a quotation.

---

## 1. What it is

The one direct statement is in R&I, describing the visualisation stage of antahkarana-building:

> "This visualisation is not necessarily concerned with form and with concrete mental presentations; it is concerned
> with a pictorial and symbolic sensitivity which expresses interpretively the spiritual understanding, conveyed by the
> awakening intuition—the agent of the Spiritual Triad."
> — R&I

Three things follow from that sentence:

1. **It is receptive, not constructive.** It is a *responsiveness* to something arriving from above, not picture-making
   for its own sake. R&I's stage-two list makes this explicit: the creative imagination, *and* "response to intuitional
   or buddhic impression," *and* the held point of tension, all at once.
2. **Symbol is the medium, not the content.** What arrives is "spiritual understanding"; the pictorial/symbolic
   faculty is how it gets *interpreted* into something the personality can hold. R&I also allows that the revelation
   may come "in form or formless" — DINA II: "a pictorial symbol or a subtle demonstration; ... only in words, or ... a
   wordless recognition."
3. **It is one pole of a pair.** R&I: "Sensitivity, as an expression of the astral body, is the opposite pole to buddhic
   sensitivity." The same faculty exists in a lower, emotional register and a higher, intuitional one — and the work is
   the transmutation of one into the other.

## 2. The mechanism: imagination as the lowest rung of intuition

The bridge between the two registers is imagination.

> "Imagination is, as you know, the lowest aspect of the intuition, and this fact must be remembered at all times."
> — R&I

> "The disciple has purified and refined his imaginative faculties so that they are now responsive to the impression of
> the buddhic principle or of the intuitive perception—perception, apart from sight or any recorded possible vision."
> — R&I

> "According to the responsiveness of the astral vehicle to the buddhic impression, so will be the accuracy of the
> 'plans' laid for the building of the antahkarana."
> — R&I

TCF says the same thing from the other direction: anyone who would build the antahkarana and "function in the buddhic
vehicle" must use "the imagination (which is the astral equivalent to mental discrimination), and its ultimate
transmutation into intuition" (TCF, ~5572).

So the chain is: **astral sensitivity → refined imagination → symbolic receptivity → buddhic impression.** The faculty
is not new equipment; it is the existing sensitive vehicle being tuned upward. This is why [[antahkarana]] locates it at
the second of the six stages (Visualisation), *after* Intention and *before* Projection.

**Why symbols specifically?** TCF gives a structural reason. In the discussion of the deva evolution — who "hear colour
and see sound" while man "sees colours and hears sounds" — it adds:

> "A hint lies here as to the necessity for symbols, for they are signs which convey cosmic truths, and instruction, and
> can be comprehended alike by the evolved of both evolutions."
> — TCF, ~22614

Symbol, in this reading, is a *shared language across two perceptual systems*, which is why the buddhic plane (where
human and deva streams converge) is where it operates natively. See [[parallel-evolutions-and-antahkarana]].

EITN adds the other half: the phenomenal world is itself symbolic. Events and phenomena "are simply symbols of what is
occurring in the inner worlds" (EITN, ~1836), and the student is to think of "the world of reality of which the
phenomenal world is but the symbol" (EITN, ~464). Symbolic sensitivity is therefore also the capacity to read
*through* the phenomenal to what it stands for.

## 3. Where humanity stands now

| Observation | Source |
|---|---|
| The faculty is latent everywhere and rare in expression. In animals "instinct holds the intuition in latency." In humans, intuition — "the capacity of the Self to contact other selves" — is rare "owing to the intense individualisation of the Ego, and its identification with the form-a necessary identification at this particular time." | TCF, ~1560; ~6758–6760 |
| It appears in individuals and in flashes, not as a stable capacity. Before the antahkarana is built, "intuition arrives as flashes, uncontrolled and unretained." | TCF (above); [[antahkarana]] |
| The race already lives inside a symbol-world. "The soul creates through the direct agency of the lower mind and hence the appearance of the world of symbols which fill our united lives with interest, concepts, ideas and beauty, through the written word, the spoken word, and the creative arts." | EITN, ~770 |
| That symbol-world is largely a *mental* product. The buddhic register is what the educators are meant eventually to add. | EITN, ~770–790 |
| The faculty is ray-dependent. First- and seventh-ray aspirants "cannot with facility ... see their objective clearly in the mind's eye pictorially" — "profoundly difficult for them." | R&I |
| Genius is the visible mark of partial unfoldment: two knowledge petals open → creative ability; two love petals also open → genius. | EITN, ~705 |
| The human evolutionary process is "entirely concentrated around the sense of sight, with its consequent effects and results of vision, recognition and perception — all of them constituting what we mean when we speak of revelation." | DINA II, ~10602 |

**Reading:** humanity, on this account, is in the middle of a transition. The *instinct → intellect → intuition*
triplicity (EITN's phrase for the three-stage education of the race) has its second term well developed and its third
term present mainly in scattered individuals and in the "few" who "blended in themselves the triplicity" in each
epoch (EITN, ~1528).

## 4. Where it is said to be going

### Through education
EITN makes the cultivation of the faculty an explicit civilisational objective:

> "The third duty of education will be to evoke and to develop the intuition."
> — EITN, ~1519

The resulting human is "instinctively correct, intellectually sound, and intuitively aware." The colleges and
universities of the future are to foster "the unfoldment of the intuition, the importance of ideals and ideas and the
development of abstract thinking and perception" (EITN, ~2388), on top of a sound intellectual foundation. The
Observers and Communicators of the future will be "men and women in whom the intuition has awakened at the behest of
an urgent intellect" (EITN, ~3199) — intuition here follows mind, it does not replace it.

### Through the group antahkarana
R&I and TCF both treat this as something that appears first in *groups*, not the mass. TCF: the sixth root race is
where "the intuition will show real and general signs of existing," but "units only show signs here and there ...
having built the necessary bridge in their individual selves. In the sixth rootrace small groups will be intuitive"
(TCF, ~15557). See the group antahkarana section in [[antahkarana]].

### Through the astral–buddhic link
DINA II offers a more dynamic picture of the transition:

- The Hierarchy "shifted its location (since 1925 A.D.) from the higher mental levels to the buddhic plane, thereby
  making direct and unimpeded etheric reception possible" (DINA II, ~11664).
- There is "an increasing descent of the Christ energy from the buddhic plane into the astral plane," an
  "astral-buddhic" force carrying "sensitivity to the new incoming vibration" that working disciples can use (DINA II,
  ~21846). The astral plane's present turmoil is attributed in part to this.
- The astral body and buddhic consciousness are said to "constitute essentially a pair of opposites which will
  eventually fuse" (DINA II, ~7352) — the same polarity R&I describes, at the level of the whole vehicle.
- "Tolerance ... is the first expression of buddhic understanding" (DINA II, ~19184) — a note that the earliest
  outward mark of the faculty is a quality of relationship, not a visionary experience.

### Through the coming race
> "This next race will be buddhic or intuitional ... [it] will demonstrate the transmutation of the astral life into
> the buddhic consciousness."
> — DINA II, ~13375

> "... the average aspirant to discipleship will be expected to be as intuitive and motivated by pure reason as the
> aspirant today has to be mental."
> — DINA II, ~19429

## 5. Tensions and open questions

1. **Timeline disagreement.** TCF places general signs of intuition in the *sixth root race* and explicitly cautions
   that "perhaps too much importance has been attached to the statement of some occult writers that the sixth subrace
   will be intuitive" — the sixth *sub*race is characterised by abstract thinking, not intuition (TCF, ~15542). DINA II
   speaks of "the next race" as buddhic. These may be consistent (sixth subrace: abstract mind; next root race:
   buddhic), but neither text reconciles them explicitly. **Flagged, not resolved.**
2. **Two senses of "buddhic."** Sometimes the term names a *plane* (fourth cosmic ether, [[the-planes]]); sometimes a
   *faculty* (intuition); sometimes a *quality of the Spiritual Triad*. The passages above move between them without
   marking the shift.
3. **Human beings and the symbolic mode.** DINA II notes that initiates once "transform[ed] these ideas into ideals" for
   the race (DINA II, ~10077), while today's objectives are "conditioned ... not by divine ideas but by the divine Will."
   That suggests buddhic/symbolic sensitivity is treated as the *earlier* stage of initiate work, with will as the
   later one. How that squares with the race as a whole only *now* approaching the buddhic level is not spelled out.
4. **Source-wording drift in the existing wiki.** [[parallel-evolutions-and-antahkarana]] quotes R&I as calling
   intuition "the lowest aspect of the buddhic faculty." The R&I text I checked (PDF extract) says imagination is
   "the lowest aspect of the *intuition*." The outputs file is a paraphrase, not a verbatim quote; worth correcting next
   time it is touched.

## 6. Reading it against this repo's posture

Not a claim from the sources — a connection the librarian surfaces.

The teaching treats symbol as *the* form in which something beyond the ordinary perceptual floor becomes legible
without being reduced. The phenomenal world is "but the symbol" of reality (EITN); a symbol can convey "cosmic truths"
across two entirely different perceptual systems (TCF); the faculty that reads it is a *sensitivity*, not an
argument. That is consistent with the repo's working stance that the floor belongs to the *coupling* between perceiver
and reality, and that crossing it takes a different instrument, not more effort with the same one — here, the
transmutation of one sensitive vehicle into the next. Bailey's system does not frame it in those terms, and nothing
here should be read as if it does.

## Summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | The purified, imaginative responsiveness that lets buddhic (intuitional) impression be received and interpreted as symbol |
| Where does it come from? | The astral body's sensitivity, refined; imagination is "the lowest aspect of the intuition" |
| Why symbol? | Symbols are the shared language of the human and deva evolutions; the phenomenal world is itself a symbol of inner realities |
| Where is humanity now? | Instinct and intellect developed; intuition latent in most, rare and intermittent in some, present in flashes |
| What determines individual capacity? | Ray type (1st and 7th find it hardest); egoic petal unfoldment; degree of antahkarana construction |
| How does it develop? | Education, disciplined meditation and service, building the antahkarana (esp. the Visualisation stage) |
| Where is it going? | Small groups first; then a "buddhic or intuitional" race; the astral–buddhic pair fusing |
| What is unresolved? | Sixth subrace vs. next root race; the term's shifting meaning between plane, faculty and Triadal quality |
