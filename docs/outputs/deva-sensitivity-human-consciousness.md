# Expansion and Refinement: Deva Sensitivity and Human Consciousness

_A synthesis drawn from [[deva-evolution]], [[agnishvattas-and-devas]], [[agnishvattas]], [[the-planes]] and [[manas]]_
_Primary source: *A Treatise on Cosmic Fire* (TCF). Line numbers (l.) refer to `raw/alice-bailey-treatise-on-cosmic-fire.txt`._

---

## The question

Can we say that the deva evolution aims at attaining **sensitivity**, the human evolution aims at attaining
**consciousness**, and certain **karmic circumstances** bring the two together as an opportunity?

Short answer: yes, with three qualifications. "Sensitivity" is a gloss, not Bailey's word. The contrast is one of
emphasis, not an absolute divide. And the karmic link is stated for specific groups, not as a general law. The sections
below set out the text and the limits of each claim.

---

## 1. The two goals: consciousness and responsiveness

TCF's central contrast between the two streams (l. 22617–22642):

> "Man is evolving the inner vision and must learn to see.
> The devas are evolving the inner hearing and must learn to hear."

> "Man aims at self-control. Devas must develop by being controlled."

> "The third type of force, that of Will... has to play equally upon and through both evolutions, but in the one it
> demonstrates as **self-consciousness**, and in the other as **constructive vibration**."

And shortly after: "As the goal of evolution for the devas is the inner hearing, it will be apparent why mantric sounds
and balanced modulations are the method of contacting them" (l. 22659).

Two further passages put *feeling* at the centre of the deva path:

- "the usual method is for the devas gradually to work themselves towards individualisation through **expansive
  feeling**" (l. 29783)
- "For the violet devas the path of attainment lies through feeling" (l. 30310)

**Reading.** The human goal is named outright: self-consciousness. The deva goal is named as inner hearing, feeling, and
the response to sound and vibration. "Sensitivity" is a fair single word for that cluster, but it is our word.

### The limit of the contrast

Devas are not without consciousness, and the text sometimes reverses the roles:

- The greater builders are "conscious workers" on the evolutionary arc (l. 20713ff; see [[deva-evolution]]).
- A footnote in TCF: "the Devas giving the consciousness and the Pitris forming the body" (l. 13866).
- The [[agnishvattas]] are themselves devas, and "the solar Pitris... could give to man his consciousness"
  (l. 26197; see [[agnishvattas-and-devas]]).

So the claim needs to be stated precisely: **self-consciousness** is the distinctive human goal (cf. l. 20731,
"Self-Consciousness (which is the distinctive characteristic of humanity)"). **Perfected responsiveness** is the deva
goal. It is not consciousness versus no consciousness.

## 2. The two methods: Expansion and Refinement

TCF names the methods:

> "Man is evolving by means of contact and experience. **He expands.**
> The devas evolve by means of the lessening of contact. **Limitation is the law for them.**" (l. 22625)

This note names the pair **Expansion and Refinement**.

_Librarian's flag: "Refinement" is our gloss on what deva limitation produces. It is not Bailey's term for the deva
method. Her word is "limitation"._

### Why "Refinement" is a defensible gloss

TCF uses refinement steadily for what happens to **substance**, and substance is deva life ("the sumtotal of manas is
pure deva essence", l. 13841):

- "the building in of finer matter into the vehicles, **both deva and human**... which have all to be purified, rarefied,
  intensified and **refined**" (l. 19533–19536)
- "the inner essence as it makes its presence felt through the form, when the form has been brought to a stage of such
  **refinement** that it becomes possible" (l. 35147–35149)
- a body "of a greater **responsiveness**, tuned to a higher key, of more adequate **refinement**" (l. 4126)

The last passage ties refinement to responsiveness, the same quality as "sensitivity" in §1. Limitation is the method;
refinement is what it produces; responsiveness is what refinement makes possible.

### Why the pair points toward synthesis

"Expansion vs. limitation" reads as an opposition. "Expansion and Refinement" reads as two halves of one movement, and
the text shows each stream eventually taking up the other's mode:

| | Human stream | Deva stream |
|---|---|---|
| Native method | Expansion: "He expands" (l. 22625) | Refinement through limitation (l. 22625) |
| Takes up the other's mode | Discipline "that will **refine** and bring his lower vehicles into subjection" (l. 5139) | Individualisation "through **expansive** feeling" (l. 29783) |
| Shared task | "finer matter into the vehicles, both deva and human" (l. 19533) | (same) |
| End point | "In the Heavenly Man these two great aspects of divinity are equally blended" (l. 22644) | (same) |

The human who refines the vehicles and the deva who expands through feeling are each borrowing the other stream's method.
That borrowing anticipates the blending in the Heavenly Man.

## 3. Karma as the meeting point

The clearest passage (l. 14719–14724):

> "the devas of the fourth ether will be soon swinging into recognised utility and that in the sixth subrace the deva
> evolution will be exceedingly prominent. The fourth plane is the plane of at-one-ment for certain deva and human
> units, and certain groups (the fourth Creative Hierarchy and the sixth Deva Hierarchy) have a **pronounced karma to
> work out together**."

This lines up with the general statement that "the two great evolutions (human and deva) find their group unity on the
buddhic plane" (l. 11382–11383).

A second passage casts **man as mediator** of karma *within* the deva world:

> "This danger is hid in the karmic enmity existing between the two great groups of devas. These groups can only be
> united by the mediator, man." (l. 33890–33891)

The context is the magician's thought-form, "where fire and water meet". The enmity is between two deva groups, not
between devas and humans. Human consciousness is what reconciles them.

There are also openings in both directions between the streams:

- Devas "who desire to pass into the human kingdom" may do so via the bird kingdom (l. 29779).
- "The Lunar Angels have to reach the plane of the Solar Angels" (SD I, 203, quoted at l. 21542).

**Reading.** The text supports the claim that *specific* karmic bonds, between named groups, on the buddhic plane, at a
given time (the sixth subrace), are where the two streams meet. It does not state that karma is the general mechanism
by which either stream gets its opportunity. That broader reading is an extrapolation.

## Summary

| Claim | Status in TCF |
|---|---|
| Humanity aims at self-consciousness | Stated (l. 22642, l. 20731) |
| Devas aim at inner hearing, feeling, responsiveness ("sensitivity") | Stated in substance; "sensitivity" is our gloss (l. 22625, l. 22659, l. 29783) |
| Human method: expansion | Stated (l. 22625) |
| Deva method: refinement | Bailey says "limitation"; "refinement" is a gloss supported by l. 19536, l. 35149, l. 4126 |
| Each stream eventually takes up the other's method | Inference from l. 5139 and l. 29783 |
| Specific group karma joins deva and human on the buddhic plane | Stated (l. 14724) |
| Man mediates karmic enmity between deva groups | Stated (l. 33890) |
| Karma is *the* general mechanism of the meeting | Not stated; extrapolation |

## Fit with this repo's posture

This is complementarity rather than contradiction. Expansion and refinement, consciousness and responsiveness, seeing
and hearing are not rival accounts. They are the two halves that the text says are "equally blended" in the Heavenly
Man. Neither stream is the floor of the other.

## Open questions

- Is there a passage in the later books (R&I, DINA) that names the deva goal in terms closer to "sensitivity"? Not
  checked here.
- What exactly is the "pronounced karma" between the fourth Creative Hierarchy and the sixth Deva Hierarchy? TCF names
  it and does not explain it.
