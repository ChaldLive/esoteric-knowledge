# The Agnishvattas — Arrival, Nature, and Destiny

*Synthesised from A Treatise on Cosmic Fire (Bailey/DK), Division B of Section Two.*

---

## I. The Reason for Their Arrival

The Agnishvattas did not arrive arbitrarily. Their descent into our solar system was the consequence of two converging
necessities: a cosmic readiness below and a karmic pressure from above.

From below: the Law governing individualisation states that only when the heart centre of a Heavenly Man "becomes
vitalised and attains a certain vibratory capacity does the individualisation of the Monads become possible under the
Law" (TCF, ~23187). The lower three planes — mental, astral, dense physical — had to reach a specific vibratory state
before they could receive the fire these entities carried. In Earth's evolutionary history, this coincided with the
middle of the third root race (the Lemurian), in the fourth round, on the third chain.

From above: Bailey identifies the precipitating cause as a cosmic sound-event. When the WORD sounded forth which
"produced desire in the Triad for self-expression, and when the sound of the lower manifestation had blended with it,
and had risen up into the Heavens, an effect was produced which caused a response in certain allied constellations" —
specifically Sirius and two of the Pleiades — which "set loose energy which swept into the solar system, carrying with
it those solar angels who rested in the Heart of God until the hour was come" (TCF, ~23742). Three constellations govern
the appropriation by the Logos of his dense body; the triangle they form is what permitted the manasic inflow, and it
still persists.

The immediate mechanism was the vitalisation of the etheric centres of the Manasaputras on the fourth cosmic etheric
plane, which produced increased activity on the systemic mental plane. Simultaneously, mind-force poured in from the
fifth cosmic plane. These two streams, contacting the inherent energy of the logoic dense physical body, produced the
egoic groups on the mental plane and thereby made individualisation possible (TCF, ~23353).

The Agnishvattas, being in a secondary pralaya, were waiting for exactly this opening. They are cosmic entities who
operate under the Law — specifically under the karma of the planetary Logos, for "they are essentially concerned with
His Life as it actuates His centres" (TCF, ~24996). They came into our scheme "on a wave of manasic energy from the head
centre of the Logos, and in the process of passing through His Heart centre" became differentiated into seven groups,
were directed as streams of energy to particular schemes, and their contact with any scheme is what produces the
manifestation of the fourth Creative Hierarchy, leading to Monads taking form in the three worlds (TCF, ~24997–25005).

## II. Their Nature

The Agnishvattas are named under many designations in the occult literature: Solar Angels, Solar Pitris, Manasadevas,
Fire Dhyanis, Lords of the Flame, Asuras, Manasaputras (Sons of Mind). Each name emphasises a different facet of the
same class of being.

Their most essential characterisation: they are **Nirvanis from a preceding Mahamanvantara** (TCF, ~23065). In the
system that preceded ours — a system in which the cosmic physical plane attained high vibratory development and "the
fires of matter blazed forth" — certain Existences attained self-consciousness by means of material evolution. The
quality they developed is described as "active intelligence." At the close of that system they entered a nirvanic
condition and are now the vehicles of Divine Mind, the dhyan-chohanic forces, the aggregate of the Ah-hi (TCF, ~23259).

They are, Bailey states, "the Heart of the Dhyan-chohanic body" — a name given in the Secret Doctrine (SD II, 96). Their
energy comes from the spiritual sun, precisely as prana comes to the etheric body from the physical sun (TCF, ~23174).
They exist in three groups, each connected with an aspect of logoic consciousness: head centre, heart centre, throat
centre, working respectively through manasic permanent atoms, causal bodies, and mental units.

Their essential substance is energised by **ahamkara** — the principle of "I-ness." This is not a trivial detail. The
Agnishvattas do not simply transmit the I-principle from outside; they are, in their very substance, constituted of it.
"In time and space, and on the mental plane, they are Man himself in essential essence" (TCF, ~23775). They embody the
"I principle" and are the producers of self-consciousness and the builders of man's body of realisation.

They represent a peculiar type of electrical force whose work is to blend and fuse. Above all else, they are the
"transmuting fires of the system" — those agents who pass the life of God through their bodies of flame as it descends
from the higher into the lower and again as it ascends from the lower into the higher (TCF, ~23632).

Much confusion exists, Bailey notes, between those Agnishvattas who _incarnated in man_ and those who merely _implanted
the spark_ of manas in animal man. The three broad groups are:

- Those who refused to incarnate (magnetic repulsion — they could not energise the forms provided)
- Those who implanted the spark of manas (and a sub-group who fanned and fed the latent flame in the best specimens of
  animal man)
- Those who took bodies and moulded the type

This threefold division actually resolves into five, making the numerical correspondence to the fifth principle exact.

## III. The Evolutionary Opportunity They Were Seeking

In the previous solar system, the Agnishvattas developed active intelligence. In this system, "the vibration of the
cosmic astral plane is becoming dominant" and through that vibration "the Sons of desire, logoic or human, can learn
certain lessons, undergo certain experiences, and add the faculty of love-wisdom to the intelligence earlier gained"
(TCF, ~23263).

The Agnishvattas enter this system as already-perfected intelligences, but they have not yet fully integrated buddhi —
the love-wisdom principle. The entire purpose of their work in this round is to achieve that integration. At the close
of this mahamanvantara, "there will be ready for manifestation in the coming third system nirvanis who will be, in very
essence, 'active intelligent love'" (TCF, ~23269). These nirvanis are the Agnishvattas, transformed by their service.

Their opportunity is therefore an act simultaneously of service and self-development. By providing the causal body, the
egoic lotus, and the seed of self-consciousness to the human units — by sacrificing their own substance for this — they
move through an experience unavailable to them in the previous system: the development of love as a cosmic principle,
not merely intelligence.

It should be noted that they could not re-enter manifestation until specific conditions were met. "The three lower
planes are synthesised with the higher four, and the devas of an earlier mahamanvantara or solar cycle are brought into
conjunction with those of a newer order who were awaiting just conditions" (TCF, ~23546). Their waiting was not passive.
They rested "in the Heart of God" — which is to say, in a state of absorbed, nirvanic potential — until the vibration of
the lower planes rose sufficient to receive them.

## IV. The Effect on the Human Units They Individualised

The intervention of the Agnishvattas produced, at the moment of individualisation, a series of precisely described
effects on the mental plane. Bailey traces four sequential events:

**First:** The vibratory impulse of the Manasadevas (fivefold) combining with the fourfold vibration inherent in the
matter of the third subplane of the mental plane produced "the ninefold egoic lotus" — at this stage tightly closed,
nine petals folded upon one another, scintillating with light, coloured faintly with the fire of manas. The petals are
formed out of the Agnishvattas' own substance (TCF, ~23939–23940).

**Second:** A triangle of fire appeared on the mental plane, circulating between the manasic permanent atom, the centre
of the egoic lotus, and the mental unit. This triangle, composed of pure electrical manasic force, constitutes the
nucleus of the antaskarana — the bridge that the disciple will later consciously rebuild to return along the path the
descending spirit originally took (TCF, ~23946–23953).

**Third:** A downflow of buddhi occurs along the line of the manasic triangle. At the very centre of the lotus, three
additional petals appear, closing over the central flame — remaining closed until the revelation of the "jewel in the
Lotus" at a later initiation. The egoic lotus now consists of twelve petals: nine in bud form and three innermost petals
completely hidden. The three permanent atoms are simultaneously enclosed within the lotus as a dimly burning triangle.
The causal body is now complete in embryonic form (TCF, ~23959–23974).

**Fourth:** The fire circulating along the manasic triangle is withdrawn to the centre of the lotus. The triangular
prototype of the antaskarana disappears. The threefold energy is centralised to generate impulse for a downflow into the
three worlds of human endeavour (TCF, ~24011–24015).

The long-term effect is that the Agnishvattas become the continuing superintendents of the human unit's evolution.
Primarily, they bring about and maintain the union of the divine Ego and the lower personal self. Secondarily, they work
through the mental units, impressing upon each atom that portion of logoic purpose which the individual can work out on
the physical plane — at first unconsciously received by the man, later recognised consciously, and after the third
initiation expressed as will (TCF, ~24056–24065).

Critically, "it is the positive force of the Manasadevas that produces initiation" (TCF, ~24067). The Hierophant, at
each initiation, passes the voltage of the higher manasic force through the Rod of Power, stimulating the egoic lotus so
that successive petals unfold, until the electric fire of spirit is manifested. The Agnishvattas' work reaches its
culmination when the jewel at the heart of the lotus stands fully revealed.

Ultimately, through the agency of the Agnishvattas, man can "become conscious without the manasic vehicle, for manas is
but the form through which a higher principle is making itself known" (TCF, ~23779). The Agnishvattas enable the very
transcendence of the principle they embody.

## V. The End of Their Journey and Their Wider Evolutionary Endeavours

Bailey is explicit that the full revelation of this subject cannot be given, and what is offered should be treated as
the outline of a mystery that will not be resolved below the level of the third initiation in its meaning, nor fully
comprehended until the fifth.

What can be stated:

**In pralaya:** When the work of a scheme is complete, or between rounds, the Agnishvattas are withdrawn from
manifestation and "return to a cosmic centre of which the logoic head centre is but a dim reflection — they return the
richer for experience" (TCF, ~25015). The Old Commentary given in TCF says:

> _"The deva shineth with added light when the virtue of the will hath entered. He garnereth colour as the reaper
> garners wheat, and storeth it up for the feeding of the multitude. Over all this deva host the mystic Goat presideth.
> Makara is, and is not, yet the link persisteth."_

The reference to Makara (Capricorn) and the "mystic Goat" is deliberate. The Agnishvattas are connected with the
initiation process, and their destiny is bound up with what lies beyond the five initiations of this system. Rounds come
and go but the Manasadevas "are forever present, but their influence is not forever felt" (TCF, ~25024).

**In the fifth round:** At its middle point, the fifth Hierarchy of Agnishvattas will rise to its full power. This will
precede the Judgment Day and will produce on a racial scale a repetition of the individual struggle to transcend mind
and live the life of Spirit — "the final Armageddon, the planetary Kurukshetra," followed by a Judgment Day in which
those in whom the manasic principle is either over-potent or under-developed will be "cast out" and "the Dragons of
Wisdom rule" (TCF, ~23841–23848). The Christ principle — buddhi — will triumph for the remainder of the manvantara.

**In the next solar system:** The present solar Angels will have a position analogous to that of the lunar Pitris now —
forming part of the divine consciousness, yet "esoterically considered as below the threshold." Man, in that future
system, will mount upward by means of the "serpent of wisdom," and by:

> _"the domination and control of the Agnishvattas, achieve something of which even the illuminated mind of the highest
> Dhyan-chohan cannot yet conceive."_ (TCF, ~23309)

This sentence is perhaps the clearest statement Bailey gives of the upper bound of what this hierarchy represents. Even
those at the summit of our current evolutionary arc cannot form a conception of what is implied. The Agnishvattas are
not merely bridge-builders for the human kingdom; they are themselves evolving toward a condition that will serve as the
platform for an order of consciousness beyond anything accessible to the highest intelligences now manifesting in this
system.

**The limit of disclosure:** The secret of buddhi — the sixth or Christ principle — as it concerns the Sons of God,
"cannot be mentioned outside initiated circles. It holds hid the possibility of egoic unfoldment, and keeps secret the
karma of the Heavenly Men, the five Kumaras" (TCF, ~23799–23802). The connection between the fifth Hierarchy, the
Kumaras, and the mystery of cosmic buddhi constitutes the innermost ring of this subject. Bailey states the full
revelation of cosmic manas and the entities who come in on that influence "may not further be revealed at this moment" —
what is given in the Secret Doctrine and supplemented in TCF will "suffice for the investigations of students for
another generation" (TCF, ~23896).
